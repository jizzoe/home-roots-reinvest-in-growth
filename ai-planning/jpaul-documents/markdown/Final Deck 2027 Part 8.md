**Slide 2**

**The Problem**

Many Haitian women entrepreneurs increase their income but struggle to sustain business growth and achieve lasting household stability.

**Through eight years of serving women entrepreneurs, HRF identified three barriers to sustained business growth:**

- High inventory costs resulting from small-volume purchasing.
- Limited working capital that constrains business expansion.
- Business profits are often used for household needs before they can be reinvested.

**Result**

Higher business income does not consistently lead to business growth or lasting household economic stability.

**Slide 3**

**The Solution**

**Reinvest-to-Grow™ Methodology**

**A simple enterprise growth methodology that helps women entrepreneurs retain and reinvest more business income to build stronger enterprises.**

The Reinvest-to-Grow™ Methodology combines three integrated elements to help entrepreneurs convert higher earnings into sustainable enterprise growth:

- **Lower business costs** through collective purchasing.
- **Expand working capital** through graduated growth financing.
- **Strengthen reinvestment behavior**through Enterprise Growth Services.

HRF developed the Reinvest-to-Grow™ Methodology to help women entrepreneurs retain and reinvest more business income. The methodology is delivered through the Enterprise Growth Platform, validated through the Enterprise Supply Hub (the first revenue-generating business unit), and replicated through standardized digital operations.

**Result**

Lower Inventory Costs → Higher Business Margins → More Income Retained → More Income Reinvested → Enterprise Growth → Greater Household Economic Stability
