# Executive Product Brief

**Executive Product Brief**

**Enterprise Growth App**

**Version 1.0 — Business Journal Module**

**Product Requirements Document Companion Brief**

## 1. Product Overview

The Enterprise Growth App is the entrepreneur-facing component of the Home Roots Foundation (HRF) Enterprise Growth Platform.

The platform is designed to support HRF's **Reinvest-to-Grow™ Methodology**, which helps women entrepreneurs strengthen their businesses by improving business management, increasing access to affordable inputs, supporting responsible growth financing, and helping entrepreneurs retain and reinvest more income into their enterprises.

Version 1 of the Enterprise Growth App focuses on the **Business Journal Module**.

The Business Journal provides entrepreneurs with a simple, mobile-first way to:

- Record sales.
- Track expenses.
- Understand profit.
- Monitor cash movement.
- Organize business information.
- Build reliable business records.

The application is intentionally designed for entrepreneurs who may have:

- Limited bookkeeping experience.
- Limited access to traditional financial services.
- Limited digital experience.
- Intermittent internet connectivity.

The goal is not to create a traditional accounting system.

The goal is to create a simple business management tool that helps entrepreneurs understand and improve their businesses.

## 2. Strategic Context

Many entrepreneurs in emerging markets operate viable businesses but lack:

- Reliable business records.
- Visibility into profitability.
- Historical business data.
- Access to appropriate financing.
- Tools for making informed decisions.

Without business information, entrepreneurs often make decisions based on intuition alone.

Without reliable business records, organizations like HRF cannot effectively:

- Provide tailored coaching.
- Measure enterprise growth.
- Understand capital needs.
- Develop responsible financing pathways.

The Enterprise Growth App addresses this gap by creating the digital foundation for entrepreneur growth.

## 3. Product Thesis

The Enterprise Growth App is built on the following hypothesis:

If entrepreneurs can easily capture business activity and understand their financial performance, they will make better business decisions, improve business discipline, and create stronger enterprises.

Over time, the information generated by the platform can support:

- Better coaching.
- Stronger monitoring and evaluation.
- Improved understanding of entrepreneur needs.
- Responsible growth financing.
- Evidence-based credit intelligence.

## 4. Version 1 Product Goal

The goal of Version 1 is:

Build and validate a reliable entrepreneur-facing Business Journal that enables small business owners to record business activity, understand performance, and create trustworthy business data.

Version 1 success means:

An entrepreneur can:

- Record business transactions.
- Understand revenue, expenses, and profit.
- Review business activity.
- Use the application despite connectivity limitations.
- Build a consistent business record.

HRF can:

- Monitor entrepreneur engagement.
- Access reliable business information.
- Improve coaching.
- Generate stronger program reporting.

## 5. Primary Users

**Primary User**

**Entrepreneur**

A woman business owner participating in HRF's entrepreneurship programs.

Typical characteristics:

- Small informal or early-stage enterprise.
- Cash-based transactions.
- Limited bookkeeping experience.
- Mobile phone access.
- Variable internet connectivity.

**Secondary Users**

**HRF Coaches**

Use information to provide better support.

**HRF Administrators**

Use information for:

- Program management.
- Reporting.
- Monitoring.
- Financing operations.

## 6. Version 1 Scope

**Included**

**Entrepreneur Mobile Application**

- Business profile.
- Sales tracking.
- Expense tracking.
- Cash movement tracking.
- Transaction history.
- Receipt capture.
- Basic business dashboard.
- Basic reporting.
- Offline functionality.
- Synchronization.
- AI-assisted organization and explanations.

**Administrative Capabilities**

- Entrepreneur management.
- Business visibility.
- Basic reporting.
- User permissions.
- Program monitoring.

**Not Included in Version 1**

The following are intentionally deferred:

- Automated credit scoring.
- Automated lending decisions.
- Marketplace functionality.
- Full inventory management.
- Digital payments.
- Supplier marketplace.
- Advanced financial modeling.

These capabilities may be added after validating the Business Journal foundation.

## 8. Product Differentiation

The Enterprise Growth App is different from traditional bookkeeping applications.

Traditional bookkeeping asks:

"What happened financially?"

The Enterprise Growth App aims to help answer:

"What happened, what does it mean, and what should the entrepreneur do next?"

The product combines:

- Simple business recordkeeping.
- AI assistance.
- Entrepreneur-friendly explanations.
- Longitudinal business data.
- Future growth financing capability.

## 9. Non-Negotiable Product Requirements

Version 1 must prioritize:

**Simplicity**

The entrepreneur should not need accounting knowledge.

**Reliability**

Business records must not be lost.

**Accessibility**

The product must work in environments with:

- Low connectivity.
- Affordable devices.
- Multiple languages.

**Trust**

Financial information must be accurate and understandable.

**Scalability**

The architecture must support future Enterprise Growth Platform capabilities.

## 10. Expected Strategic Outcome

Successful completion of Version 1 will allow HRF to demonstrate:

- A functioning technology product.
- Entrepreneur adoption.
- Digital transformation of program delivery.
- A scalable operating model.
- A foundation for future growth financing infrastructure.

The Business Journal Module represents the first step toward building digital infrastructure that helps entrepreneurs move from survival-oriented businesses toward stronger, more resilient enterprises.
