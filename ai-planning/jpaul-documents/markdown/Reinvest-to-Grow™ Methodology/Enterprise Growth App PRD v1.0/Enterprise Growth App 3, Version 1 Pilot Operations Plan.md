# Enterprise Growth App 3, Version 1 Pilot Operations Plan

**Document 3**

**Enterprise Growth App**

**Version 1 Pilot Operations Plan**

**Purpose**

The pilot will validate whether the Enterprise Growth App creates meaningful value for entrepreneurs and HRF operations.

The pilot is both:

- A technology validation process.
- A product-learning process.

## 1. Pilot Objective

Determine whether entrepreneurs can successfully use the Business Journal Module to:

- Record business activity.
- Understand business performance.
- Improve business management habits.

## 2. Pilot Participants

Recommended initial cohort:

25–50 entrepreneurs.

Selection should include:

- Different business types.
- Different business maturity levels.
- Different digital experience levels.
- Different locations.

Do not select only highly technology-comfortable users.

## 3. Pilot Preparation

Before launch:

**Product Preparation**

Complete:

- Testing.
- Security review.
- Bug resolution.
- Reporting setup.

**Staff Preparation**

Train:

- Coaches.
- Administrators.
- Support personnel.

Staff should understand:

- How the app works.
- Common problems.
- How to support entrepreneurs.

**Entrepreneur Preparation**

Provide:

- Introduction.
- Account setup.
- First transaction training.
- Support instructions.

## 4. Pilot Timeline

**Month 1: Introduction**

Objectives:

- Create accounts.
- Complete onboarding.
- Record first transactions.
- Identify usability issues.

**Month 2: Habit Formation**

Objectives:

- Encourage consistent recording.
- Test dashboards.
- Collect feedback.

**Month 3: Learning & Improvement**

Objectives:

- Analyze usage.
- Improve workflows.
- Identify highest-value features.

**Month 4: Evaluation**

Objectives:

- Determine readiness for expansion.
- Prioritize improvements.
- Create next-version roadmap.

## 5. Pilot Metrics

**Adoption**

Track:

- Registered users.
- Activated users.
- Weekly active users.
- Retention.

**Usage**

Track:

- Transactions recorded.
- Recording frequency.
- Dashboard usage.
- Report usage.

**Quality**

Track:

- Data accuracy.
- Synchronization success.
- Errors.
- AI corrections.

**Value**

Measure:

- Entrepreneur understanding.
- Perceived usefulness.
- Coaching usefulness.

## 6. Entrepreneur Feedback Process

Collect feedback through:

**Interviews**

Questions:

- What do you like?
- What is difficult?
- What saves you time?
- What do you wish existed?

**Observation**

Observe:

- How entrepreneurs actually use the app.
- Where they struggle.
- What they ignore.

**Surveys**

Measure:

- Ease of use.
- Confidence.
- Usefulness.

## 7. Pilot Support Process

Establish:

**Technical Support**

For:

- Bugs.
- Account issues.
- Synchronization problems.

**Program Support**

For:

- Training.
- Adoption.
- Business questions.

## 8. Pilot Risks

**Low Usage**

Possible causes:

- Lack of habit.
- Complexity.
- Limited perceived value.

Response:

Simplify workflows and strengthen coaching integration.

**Data Quality Problems**

Possible causes:

- Confusion.
- Inconsistent recording.

Response:

Improve guidance and AI assistance.

**Technical Issues**

Possible causes:

- Connectivity.
- Device limitations.

Response:

Prioritize offline reliability.

## 9. Pilot Evaluation Decision

After the pilot, HRF should decide:

**Continue Scaling**

If:

- Entrepreneurs use the system.
- Data quality is acceptable.
- Product creates value.

**Modify Product**

If:

- Core value exists but workflows need improvement.

**Reconsider Features**

If:

- Features create complexity without value.

## 10. Strategic Pilot Outcome

The pilot should produce evidence that HRF has moved from:

**Concept**

↓

**Prototype**

↓

**Validated Product**

↓

**Scalable Enterprise Growth Platform**

**Final recommendation**

With these three additions, I would now freeze the PRD at **Version 1.0**.

The next work should no longer be writing requirements. It should be execution:

- Give engineer the package.
- Review architecture decisions.
- Build Version 1.
- Begin entrepreneur pilot preparation.
- Start collecting real business data.

You now have a credible product story for Miller Center, Global Good Fund, funders, and strategic partners:

"Home Roots Foundation is developing the first digital infrastructure layer of its Reinvest-to-Grow™ Methodology, beginning with an entrepreneur Business Journal that transforms informal business activity into actionable business intelligence and creates the foundation for responsible growth financing."

That is a much stronger story than simply saying "we built a bookkeeping app."
