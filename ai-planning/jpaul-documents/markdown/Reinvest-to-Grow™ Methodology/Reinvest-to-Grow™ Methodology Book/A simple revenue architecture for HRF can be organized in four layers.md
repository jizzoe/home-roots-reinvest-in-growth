**The simple HRF story**

**HRF makes money in three ways:**

- **by earning margin on the Supply Hub,**
- **by charging for selected business services,**
- **and eventually by monetizing the platform and network it builds.**

That gives Miller a much stronger answer than saying the organization depends on grants alone.

**How the Supply Hub fits**

The Supply Hub should be presented as:

- the **first earned-revenue engine**,
- the **current validation site**,
- and the **proof point** for the broader model.

In other words, the Hub is not the whole business model. It is the **beachhead revenue engine** that proves the methodology can create value.

**How to explain the business model in one line**

**HRF earns revenue by helping women entrepreneurs lower costs, access appropriate capital, and use enterprise support; the organization captures value through Supply Hub margin, financing revenue, service fees, and later platform revenue.**

**A cleaner Miller-friendly framing**

For Miller Center, I would present the revenue architecture this way:

**Current**

- Supply Hub margin
- Financing revenue
- Mobile money / transaction revenue

**Near-term**

- Business service fees
- Procurement support fees
- Coaching or diagnostic packages for partner groups

**Longer-term**

- Platform participation fees
- Licensing
- Data/reporting services
- Supplier partnership revenue

**Capital to support growth**

- Grants
- Recoverable grants
- PRIs
- Concessionary debt

**Why this is stronger**

It answers the Miller question, “How does HRF make money?” with a much better answer:

**Not from one source, but from an earned-revenue stack that starts with the Supply Hub and expands into services, platform income, and partnerships.**

That is a much more resilient model than depending on one revenue stream.

**Best strategic principle**

HRF should not look like a grant-dependent nonprofit trying to become profitable overnight.

It should look like an organization that is:

- **building a real business unit now,**
- **adding service revenue next,**
- **and creating a scalable platform later.**
