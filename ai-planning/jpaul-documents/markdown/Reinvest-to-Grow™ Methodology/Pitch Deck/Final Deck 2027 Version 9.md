## Slide 2 — The Problem

Many Haitian women entrepreneurs generate income but still struggle to build durable businesses.

The challenge is not simply low income.

The deeper problem is that business income is often lost before it can be converted into enterprise growth.

**Main barriers**

- High inventory costs
- Limited working capital
- Thin margins
- Household financial pressure
- Inadequate support for productive reinvestment

**Result** Higher income does not consistently lead to stronger enterprises or lasting household economic stability.

## Slide 3 — The Reinvest-to-Grow™ Theory

Reinvest-to-Grow™ is HRF’s enterprise development methodology.

It is built on a simple proposition:

**If women entrepreneurs can retain more business income, productively reinvest more of that income, and strengthen their enterprise capability, they can build stronger, more productive, and more resilient businesses over time.**

**Core idea** Retained income is the bridge between current business activity and future enterprise growth.

**Important** Not every entrepreneur will graduate to SME status. The model is designed for **differentiated outcomes**, including strengthening, growth, and selective graduation.
